Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy describes how Mirrorball Fantasy League handles information when you use its website or beta app. It is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Dancing with the Stars.

Information we use

  • Account information: your email address, sign-in provider, username, display name, and optional profile picture. If you use an email/password account, authentication is handled by Supabase; the site does not store your password in its league tables.
  • League activity: leagues you join or create, fantasy-team names, draft picks, roster assignments, trades, invitations, weekly scores, and related history needed to run the game.
  • Technical information: sign-in sessions and browser storage needed to keep you signed in, remember your selected league, and cache public dance photos. Our hosting and authentication providers may process ordinary request and diagnostic information needed to deliver their services.
  • Feedback: information you choose to send to our support email or through TestFlight feedback.

How we use and show it

We use this information to authenticate you, maintain fantasy leagues, show standings and dance history, support trades and invitations, respond to requests, and protect the service. Your username, display name, optional profile picture, fantasy-team name, and game activity may be visible to other members of your leagues. Some league and show information is also visible on the public website. Do not use a name or photo you do not want others to see.

Service providers

Supabase hosts authentication, league data, and uploaded profile pictures. GitHub Pages hosts the website and public dance images. Apple or Google handles sign-in if you choose that provider. TestFlight and Apple may process beta testing, diagnostics, and feedback according to their own policies. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

The site uses browser storage for your session and limited interface preferences or caches. We retain account and league information while it is needed to operate the service and its season history. If you delete your account from the profile editor, your sign-in, personal profile, invitations, and uploaded profile pictures are removed. A team can remain in its league without a manager, with its scores retained for that season; the team is removed after the season ends. Some information may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or logs according to their retention practices.

Your choices

You can edit your username, display name, and profile picture in your account profile. You can delete your account there using the deletion confirmation. To ask about your information or request help, email mirrorball-fantasy@codys-server.net.

Updates

We may update this policy as the beta changes. The effective date above will change when we do. For privacy questions, contact mirrorball-fantasy@codys-server.net.
