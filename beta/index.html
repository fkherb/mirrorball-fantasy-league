Help test Mirrorball Fantasy League

Mirrorball Fantasy League is a fan-made fantasy game for Dancing with the Stars. Create or join a small league, draft a cast roster with friends, follow dances and judges’ scores, and see how your fantasy team performs each week. This beta is for testing the sign-in, league, draft, roster, trade, and weekly-results experience. It is not affiliated with the show.

What to try

  • Sign in and open your league and fantasy team.
  • Explore the cast roster, dances, weekly scores, and standings.
  • Try league invitations, roster changes, and trades when available in your league.
  • Tell us about anything confusing, broken, or difficult to use.

Feedback and questions
